Meeshy 1.1.8 — calls that look better, read clearer, in your language.

• Redesigned calls: every button is glass (Liquid Glass on iOS 26), whoever speaks lights up in their colour, and in landscape up to four people fit on one row.
• Captions: each sentence shows its language and unfolds its original with a tap. After the call, the transcript unfolds under its bubble in the conversation.
• Recording and screen sharing: everyone sees who asks and who agrees, recording starts as Audio or Video, and "Stop sharing" is always within reach.
• Creating: paste an image to set the scene background, paste text to fill the caption; the viewfinder offers Text, Background, Gallery and Sticker first.
• Simpler everywhere: sign-up, settings and the game say each thing once, with the details waiting behind an (i).

And of course, fixes and smoother performance throughout.

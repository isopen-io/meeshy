A second account is required to receive a call. Please contact us if the one below is missing.

VOIP / CALLKIT (UIBackgroundModes "voip")
A call needs two accounts: one to place it, one to receive it.
1. Sign in on device A with the first account, on device B with the second.
2. On device A, open the Contacts tab, select the second account, tap the
   phone or video icon.
3. Device B rings through CallKit — the native full-screen incoming-call UI
   appears even when the app is backgrounded or force-quit. It is delivered
   by a PushKit VoIP push and reported with CXProvider.reportNewIncomingCall.
4. Answer, then press Home on device B: audio keeps flowing, which is what
   the "audio" background mode is used for during a call.

PERSISTENT AUDIO (UIBackgroundModes "audio")
1. Open any conversation and tap the microphone to record a short voice
   message, or open an existing one.
2. Tap play, then press Home to go to the Home Screen.
3. Playback continues, and the Now Playing widget appears on the Lock
   Screen and in Control Center with play/pause and scrub controls
   (MPNowPlayingInfoCenter + MPRemoteCommandCenter).
4. The same applies to audio stories and reels in the social feed.

Neither background mode is used for keep-alive purposes.

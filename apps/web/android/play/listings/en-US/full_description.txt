Somewhere on this planet, there's a best friend who doesn't speak your language. Not yet.

Meeshy is the messaging app where you make friends all over the world without ever feeling the language barrier. You write in your language, they read in theirs. You send a voice message in English, they hear it in Korean. No copy-pasting into a translator: everyone speaks their own language, and everyone understands each other.

THE WHOLE WORLD IN YOUR GROUP CHAT
• Chat one-on-one or in groups: everyone reads messages in their own language
• 76 translatable languages, translated automatically: from Korean to Japanese, Spanish to Arabic, Swahili to Lingala
• The original is always within reach: you can see what was really written
• Reactions, replies, mentions: a real messaging app, fully translated

YOUR VOICE MESSAGES CROSS BORDERS
• Send a voice message: your friends can listen to it translated into their language
• Every voice message comes with its transcript, translated too

AUDIO AND VIDEO CALLS WITH LIVE CAPTIONS
• Call anyone, one-on-one or in a group: their words appear as captions in your language, in real time
• Incoming calls ring full screen, even when the app is closed
• Video calls keep going in a floating window when you switch apps

MUCH MORE THAN CHAT
• A feed of posts, stories, reels and statuses from friends around the world, translated too
• Communities to find your people: K-pop, anime, gaming, football, music
• Search for people and send them a friend request
• Your progress: levels, challenges, badges and achievements as you connect

ZERO FRICTION
• Invite anyone with a simple link: they can join your conversation without creating an account
• Share photos, videos, your location, contacts and stickers
• Send disappearing, blurred or view-once messages

YOUR DATA BELONGS TO YOU
• Export your data or delete your account anytime, right from the app
• No ads

Whether you're learning Korean because of your favorite band, looking for a pen pal in Tokyo, gaming with a team in Brazil or your family lives on three continents, Meeshy makes distance and language disappear.
